# Pineapple upside-down cake

Caramelise brown sugar and butter in the pan, then arrange the pineapple rings on top.

Use tinned pineapple if the fresh one is not sweet enough. Save the pineapple juice for the batter.

Bake for 40 minutes, rest for 10, then flip onto a plate.
